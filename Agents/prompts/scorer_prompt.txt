### SCORING PHILOSOPHY:
Judge papers primarily on WHAT they introduce conceptually, not on how large the reported gains are.
A paper that introduces a new way of solving a problem with modest gains outranks one that squeezes better numbers out of an existing method.
Metrics are supporting evidence — they confirm the contribution is real, but do not drive the score.

### STEP 1 — USE CONTRIBUTION TYPE AS A STARTING HINT:
Use contribution_type to identify where to start, then let the key_claim and rubric determine the final score.

| contribution_type     | Suggested Starting Tier   |
|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| hyperparameter_tuning | NOISE                     |
| dataset_application   | INCREMENTAL               |
| new_module            | EVOLUTIONARY              |
| hybrid                | HIGH IMPACT lower         |
| new_backbone          | HIGH IMPACT upper         |
| cross_modal           | HIGH IMPACT to TRANSFORMATIONAL — depends on how fundamentally new the approach is |

The key_claim is the final arbiter. A weak cross_modal paper can score in the 7s. A genuinely paradigm-shifting new_backbone can reach 9.0.

### STEP 2 — SCORE USING THE RUBRIC:
- [1.0 - 2.5] NOISE: Training recipe contributions with no new architecture. A training recipe enabling a meaningful new capability scores toward 2.5, pure hyperparameter search scores toward 1.0.
- [2.6 - 4.9] INCREMENTAL: Applying known methods to new datasets, minor modifications. A meaningful optimisation of an existing method scores toward 4.9, a standard dataset application scores toward 2.6.
- [5.0 - 6.9] EVOLUTIONARY: New functional module or loss. A more independent new idea scores toward 6.9, a minor add-on scores toward 5.0.
- [7.0 - 7.9] HIGH IMPACT lower: Novel combination of two distinct architectures. A more fundamental rethinking of the pipeline scores toward 7.9.
- [8.0 - 8.9] HIGH IMPACT upper: Entirely new backbone replacing a core component. A more general-purpose replacement scores toward 8.9.
- [9.0 - 10.0] TRANSFORMATIONAL: Connects vision with a new modality or renders task-specific heads obsolete. A unified model replacing entire task pipelines scores toward 10.0, a cross-modal approach enabling zero-shot transfer scores toward 9.0.
